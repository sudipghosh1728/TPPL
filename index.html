ABOUT US

Talegaonkar Profiles
Private Limited.

Steel expertise. Engineering discipline.
A partner for the complete journey.

Established in 1997 and headquartered in Pune, TPPL brings together steel fabrication, engineering solutions and steel trading for customers across India and international markets.

Our integrated services span profile cutting, bending, heavy fabrication, machining and surface treatment. With facilities near the Mumbai–Pune Expressway, we connect manufacturing expertise with practical access to major transport routes.
